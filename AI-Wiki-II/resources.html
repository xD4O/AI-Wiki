The master list — papers, docs, Hugging Face models, leaderboards, blogs, podcasts, and awesome-lists referenced throughout Volume II. Verified April 2026.

State of the art, April 2026
  • Frontier closed: Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 (1M context), OpenAI GPT-5 series, Google Gemini 3.1 Pro.
  • Top open-weight: Alibaba Qwen 2.5 / QwQ / Qwen 3.x; DeepSeek V3/R1 family; Moonshot Kimi K2; Meta Llama 3.3/4; Google Gemma 3; Mistral Large; Microsoft Phi-4.
  • Local runtimes: Ollama (now MLX on Apple Silicon), LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp, SGLang.
  • Benchmarks migrated: SWE-bench Pro, τ2-bench, GAIA, OSWorld-Verified have largely replaced SWE-bench Verified at the frontier.
  • Frameworks: LangGraph overtook CrewAI in early 2026; smolagents leads the lightweight tier.
  • MCP: Spec 2025-11-25; governance now in the Agentic AI Foundation; 2026 focus is enterprise auth + transport scale.
