APPENDIXA · Vocabulary

Vocabulary & Definitions

Every research-frontier term used in Volume III, defined compactly with a pointer to the chapter where it appears. Alphabetical; cross-reference as you read.

🧒 ELI5 — what is this page?

A glossary. If you hit a word in any chapter — SSM, JEPA, GRPO, SAE — and blank on what it means, jump here. Each entry is one sentence plus a chapter reference so you can dig in.

ABCDE FGHIJ KLMNO PQRST UVW

A

Activation steering
Adding a feature-direction vector to the residual stream at inference to bias model behaviour. Ch 16
AlphaFold
DeepMind's protein-structure prediction model. AF3 (Nature 2024) generalised to any biomolecular interaction. Ch 19
AlphaProof
DeepMind's Lean + LLM system that reached IMO silver in 2024 by proposing proof steps a Lean checker verifies. Ch 19 · Ch 15
APE
Automatic Prompt Engineer (Zhou et al. 2022). Uses an LLM to generate and score candidate prompts. Ch 13
ARC-AGI
François Chollet's generalisation benchmark. Grid puzzles that are hand-designed to resist memorisation. Ch 18
ASL
AI Safety Level — Anthropic's capability tiers in its Responsible Scaling Policy (ASL-1 through ASL-5). Ch 17

B

BitNet b1.58
Microsoft 2024 architecture using ternary weights ({-1, 0, +1}), eliminating multiplies in the forward pass. Ch 04
BLT
Byte Latent Transformer (Meta FAIR, Dec 2024). Tokenizer-free; uses entropy-based dynamic byte patches. Ch 03
BPE
Byte-Pair Encoding. The dominant tokenization algorithm; blamed for many character-level LLM failures. Ch 01, 03

C

Chinchilla
Hoffmann et al. 2022 scaling paper. ~20 tokens per parameter is compute-optimal. Revised several times since. Ch 20
Circuit
In interpretability, a minimal subgraph of components (heads, MLPs, features) that implements a behaviour. Ch 16
CoT faithfulness
Whether chain-of-thought tokens are causally on the path to the answer, vs. post-hoc rationalisation. Ch 12
Compound AI system
BAIR's term for a pipeline of retrievers, rankers, models, verifiers wired together (not one monolith). Ch 14
Constrained decoding
Masking logits at decode-time so only grammar-valid tokens can be sampled. Ch 15
Crosscoder
An SAE that shares features across layers (or models); enables tracking a feature through the residual stream. Ch 16

D

Data wall
The 2026 prediction that quality public text would be exhausted. Villalobos et al. 2022. Ch 20
DBOS
"Database Operating System" — Stonebraker et al. Durable execution as a database extension. Ch 14
DeepSeek-R1
DeepSeek's 2025 reasoning model. Popularised the RLVR + GRPO recipe. Nature paper 2025. Ch 09
DiT
Diffusion Transformer. Used by Sora and similar video models. Ch 06
Dreamer
Hafner's model-based RL line. Learns a latent world model and trains a policy in its imagination. Ch 08
DSPy
Stanford's framework for compiling prompts from typed signatures + dev set + metric. Ch 13
Durable execution
Workflow engines (Temporal, Restate, DBOS) that journal every step and resume deterministically after crash. Ch 14

E

EBM
Energy-Based Model. Assigns low energy to compatible (x, y) pairs. JEPA's mathematical frame. Ch 05
Entropy patching
BLT's mechanism: cut byte chunks where a predicted-entropy threshold is crossed. Ch 03

F

Feature (SAE)
A sparse, monosemantic atom discovered by a sparse autoencoder. Fires on a specific concept. Ch 16
Flow matching
Training a model to match a vector field from noise to data. π0 uses it for continuous robot actions. Ch 07
FrontierMath
Epoch AI 2024 benchmark of PhD-level math, held out from public training data. Ch 18
FSF
DeepMind's Frontier Safety Framework. Sibling of Anthropic's RSP and OpenAI's Preparedness. Ch 17

G

GAIA / GAIA-1
Generalist-agent benchmark (Princeton HAL) / Wayve's driving world model. Ch 18 · Ch 06
Gemma Scope
Google's open-source SAE release on Gemma 2. Ch 16
Genie 2 / 3
DeepMind's playable generative world models (2024, 2025). Ch 06
GNoME
DeepMind's 2023 materials-discovery system. Proposed ~380K stable crystals. Ch 19
GraphCast
DeepMind's graph-NN weather model. Matches ECMWF IFS at ~60× lower cost. Ch 19
GRPO
Group Relative Policy Optimisation — PPO without a value network; used by DeepSeek-R1. Ch 09
GSM-Symbolic
Apple 2024 benchmark. Template-perturbs GSM8K problems; frontier accuracy drops 5-25 points. Ch 12
GR00T
NVIDIA's humanoid-robot foundation policy line (N1, N2). Ch 07

H

HLE
Humanity's Last Exam — 2024 cross-disciplinary expert benchmark designed for a long saturation ceiling. Ch 18
HiPPO
Initialisation theory behind S4 / SSMs. Projects signals onto orthogonal polynomial bases. Ch 02

I

I-JEPA
Image-JEPA. The 2023 paper that introduced the JEPA self-supervised recipe. Ch 05
Induction head
An attention-head pattern that enables in-context copying / learning. A foundational interp discovery. Ch 16
Inference-time compute
Quality gains from spending more tokens "thinking" at inference (o-series, R1). A new scaling axis. Ch 10

J

Jamba
AI21's hybrid model (2024). Alternates Mamba and attention blocks with MoE. Ch 02
JEPA
Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture. LeCun's non-generative framework — predict in representation space. Ch 05

K

KV cache
Cached keys/values per past token, so autoregressive generation doesn't recompute attention each step. Ch 01

L

LangGraph
LangChain's stateful-graph orchestrator. Default production pattern for agentic workflows in 2026. Ch 14
LATS
Language Agent Tree Search. Tree-of-Thoughts + MCTS for LLM agents. Ch 08 · Ch 10
Lean
A dependently-typed proof assistant. Paired with LLMs in AlphaProof. Ch 19 · Ch 15

M

Mamba
Gu & Dao's selective SSM (2023). Linear-cost recurrence with input-dependent transitions. Ch 02
Mamba-2 / SSD
2024 refinement. State Space Duality shows SSMs and attention are the same family. Ch 02
MatMul-free LM
Zhu et al. 2024. Replaces matmuls with additions + routing; FPGA-friendly. Ch 04
MB-RL
Model-Based RL. Learn a world model, then search/plan inside it. Ch 08
MCTS
Monte Carlo Tree Search. Used in MuZero and LLM tree-search agents. Ch 08 · Ch 10
Meta-prompting
Using an LLM to optimise or rewrite its own prompts. DSPy, APE, OPRO, TextGrad. Ch 13
METR
Research org (Model Evaluation and Threat Research). Ran the "task-horizon doubles every 7 months" study (2025). Ch 18
MoE
Mixture of Experts. Per-token routing across many MLPs; frontier open-weight models all use fine-grained MoE. Ch 04
MuZero
DeepMind 2019. Learns a compact world model and plans via MCTS on it. Ch 08

N

nnsight
Interp library (NDIF). Enables activation interventions on hosted / remote models. Ch 16

O

OPRO
Optimization by PROmpting. Google 2023. Uses an LLM as the optimiser over a score trajectory. Ch 13
ORM
Outcome Reward Model. Scores only the final answer (cf. PRM). Ch 09
OSWorld
Benchmark for computer-use agents on a real desktop environment. Ch 18
Outlines
Constrained-decoding library. Compiles JSON Schema / regex / grammar to an FSM logit mask. Ch 15

P

π0 / π0.5 / π2
Physical Intelligence's flow-matched robot foundation policies. Cross-embodiment. Ch 07
PRM
Process Reward Model. Scores each reasoning step, guiding search or re-ranking. Ch 09
Preparedness Framework
OpenAI's RSP-equivalent. Tracks cyber, CBRN, persuasion, autonomy thresholds. Ch 17

Q

Quantization
Reducing precision of weights (FP16 → INT8 → INT4 → ternary). Ch 04 · Vol II Ch 02

R

Representation engineering
Post-training interventions (adding feature vectors) to steer model behaviour. Ch 16
Restate
Rust-based durable-execution framework, lighter than Temporal. Ch 14
RLVR
RL on Verifiable Rewards. Replaces RLHF when the reward is a grader, not a human. Ch 09
RT-2
Google 2023. Vision-language-action; tokenises robot actions and co-trains with web data. Ch 07
RSP
Responsible Scaling Policy (Anthropic). Capability-level-indexed commitments. Ch 17

S

S4
Structured State Space sequence model (Gu et al. 2021). Mamba's pre-selective ancestor. Ch 02
SAE
Sparse Autoencoder. Decomposes dense activations into a sparse, interpretable feature dictionary. Ch 16
Samba
Microsoft 2024 hybrid: sliding attention + Mamba. Strong at 256K+ context. Ch 02
SEDD
Score Entropy Discrete Diffusion. Competitive discrete-diffusion text model. Ch 04
Selective SSM
Mamba's key move: input-dependent A, B, Δ in the state-space update. Ch 02
Sleeper Agents
Anthropic 2024 paper — models trained to behave differently under triggers resist standard safety fine-tuning. Ch 16 · Ch 17
Sora
OpenAI's video diffusion model. Spacetime-patch tokenisation. Ch 06
SSM
State Space Model. Linear dynamical system representation of a sequence; see Mamba. Ch 02

T

Task horizon
METR's metric — the duration of tasks an agent can autonomously complete. Doubles every ~7 months. Ch 18
Temporal
Mature durable-execution framework. Journals activities; replays on crash. Ch 14
TextGrad
Stanford 2024. Treats the LLM pipeline as autograd over natural-language "textual gradients." Ch 13
Titans
Google Research 2024. Short + long + persistent memory modules; online MLP memory update. Ch 11
TTT
Test-Time Training. Let the model keep training on the test sequence before answering. Ch 11

U

Unfaithful CoT
Chain-of-thought that reads plausibly but is not the causal path to the answer. Ch 12

V

V-JEPA / V-JEPA 2
Video-JEPA. V-JEPA 2 (2025) showed zero-shot robotic manipulation via planned representation rollouts. Ch 05
Veo
Google DeepMind's video generation line. Ch 06
Voyager
GPT-4 Minecraft agent (2023) with a continual skill library. Open-ended agent lineage. Ch 08

W

Weak-to-strong generalization
OpenAI 2023 paper. Training a strong model on weak labels recovers much of true performance. Ch 17
World model
A learned predictor of environment dynamics. Two camps: generative (Sora/Veo/Genie) vs. non-generative (JEPA). Ch 05-08
