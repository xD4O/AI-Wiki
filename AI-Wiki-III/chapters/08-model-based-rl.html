CHAPTER 08Part II · World Models

Model-Based RL & Planning

The other family of world-model thinking — not "generate a video that looks right" but "build a compact predictor, then search inside it." Dreamer, MuZero, and the tree-search lineage that quietly powers 2026's reasoning agents.

2016 AlphaGo 2019 MuZero 2023 Dreamer-V3 2023 Voyager 2024 OMNI-EPIC (Clune) 2025 tree search inside LLM reasoners
🧒 ELI5 — explain like I'm five

Two ways to get good at chess. Method A: play a million games and build up instinct. Method B: learn how the pieces move, then when your turn comes, imagine the next few moves in your head and pick the best one.

Most AI today uses Method A ("model-free RL"). Model-based RL is Method B: learn a world model (how things change when you act), then search inside it. Dreamer and MuZero are Method B success stories.

Why care in 2026? Because when an LLM "thinks before answering," it's starting to look a lot like Method B.

8.1 Dreamer — Imagining in Latent Space

Hafner's Dreamer line (V1 → V3) learns three things jointly:

  • A representation of the environment's state from raw observations.
  • A dynamics model: given state + action, predict next state.
  • A policy + value trained by imagining rollouts inside the dynamics model — no extra environment interaction.

Dreamer-V3 (2023) was the first to master Minecraft diamond collection from scratch without task-specific tuning — a long-horizon exploration problem that prior model-free RL had failed on. The recipe works across 150+ tasks with one set of hyperparameters.

Dreamer-V3: a world model learned online, a policy learned inside its imagination. Sample-efficient RL at scale.

8.2 MuZero — Planning in a Learned Model

DeepMind's MuZero (2019) learned a compact dynamics model (enough to predict rewards and values) and did MCTS on top — mastering Go, chess, shogi, and 57 Atari games with one algorithm. The key insight: you don't need a pixel-accurate world model for planning; you need a reward-accurate one.

MuZero was the last "board game" milestone before the field pivoted to LLM agents. Its DNA is visible in every LLM tree-search system.

8.3 LLM Tree Search as Descendants

Vol II Ch 08 introduced LATS and Tree-of-Thoughts at a practitioner level. From a model-based-RL framing:

  • The world model is the LLM's next-state-given-actions prediction.
  • The value function is a reward model or self-consistency score.
  • The search is MCTS or beam search over "thought" nodes.

This lineage matters in 2026 because inference-time compute (Ch 10) is essentially MB-RL-style planning adapted to language. The techniques are old; the substrate is new.

8.4 Open-Ended Agents

Jeff Clune's line — Voyager (Minecraft GPT-4 agent), OMNI-EPIC (2024), Open-Ended Learning — pushes beyond "solve this task" toward "generate interesting tasks and solve them." The research bet: the path from current agents to AGI is not a benchmark to saturate but a process that keeps generating new benchmarks for itself.

Planning is what agents need

Direct-policy RL plateaus. MB-RL + search has the best sample efficiency. Every LLM reasoning breakthrough since 2024 has added some form of search. The path forward is richer world models and better planners, not bigger policies.

Just scale the policy

Dreamer and MuZero were beautiful papers but didn't ship in production. Frontier LLMs do reasoning with a single forward pass (for non-reasoning models) or implicit CoT (for reasoning models). Planning-in-a-learned-model adds complexity that scale has rendered optional.

❓ Open questions

  • Is there a hybrid where an LLM is the world model and RL is done in its imagination space (Dreamer-of-language)?
  • Do open-ended agents generalise to new task distributions, or just expand within their generator's prior?
  • What's the sample-efficiency delta between RL on verifiable rewards (Ch 09) and Dreamer-style MB-RL, at the same compute?
