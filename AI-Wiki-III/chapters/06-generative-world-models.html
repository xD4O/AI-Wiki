CHAPTER 06Part II · World Models

Generative World Models — Sora, Veo, Genie

The other world-model camp. If JEPA (Ch 05) predicts compressed representations, the generative camp bets the opposite: generate the full world, extract understanding as a side-effect.

2023-09 GAIA-1 (Wayve) 2024-02 Sora (OpenAI) 2024-05 Veo (Google) 2024-12 Genie 2 (DeepMind) 2025 Veo 3 · Genie 3 2026 playable world models in training loops
🧒 ELI5 — explain like I'm five

Imagine a video game engine that can draw any world — not from a fixed game file, but from a sentence: "a cat skateboarding down a Tokyo alley." Models like Sora do that: text or image in, video out.

The interesting claim: to draw the cat realistically, the model had to learn some things about physics, gravity, lighting, how legs move. Not perfectly, but enough that maybe — just maybe — we can use that learned "mental video game" for planning, like letting a robot imagine its next move before taking it.

6.1 Sora — Spacetime Patches & the Diffusion Transformer

OpenAI's Sora technical report (Feb 2024) introduces two ideas:

  • Spacetime patches. A video is divided into (T, H, W) patches — small cubes of pixels. Each patch becomes a token.
  • Diffusion Transformer (DiT). A transformer denoises these patch-tokens rather than image pixels directly. This scales: Sora is trained on O(10⁴) TPU/GPU-hours and produces a minute of coherent video.

The paper's bold subtitle is "Video generation models as world simulators." OpenAI's claim: Sora implicitly learns physics, persistence of identity, scene composition.

Sora's pipeline: raw video → spacetime patch tokens → diffusion transformer denoises them → decode back to pixels.

6.2 Veo, Genie, and the DeepMind Line

Veo 2/3 (Google DeepMind 2024-2025) — Google's counterpart to Sora. Similar architecture, larger training set, better physical plausibility in comparative studies.

Genie 2 (DeepMind Dec 2024) — a playable world model. Unlike Sora which generates video given a prompt, Genie takes a single image and produces an interactive environment with latent actions. Press "left," the world responds; press "right," the world responds differently. Closer to a learned game engine than a video model.

Genie 3 (2025) — scales Genie 2 to hours-long playable generated worlds, moving toward the "learned simulator as training data" loop.

6.3 World Models for RL — GAIA-1, Dreamer

The older lineage is Dreamer (Hafner et al.) — train a world model in a latent space, then do RL inside the model's imagination. 2024's Dreamer-V3 was the first to master Minecraft diamond-collection from scratch with a general recipe.

GAIA-1 (Wayve, 2023) — a generative driving world model, used internally for training self-driving policies without real-world miles.

We dig into MB-RL properly in Ch 08.

6.4 Does Generation Imply Understanding?

Yes — the Sora thesis

Sora routinely generates plausible reflections, shadows, and physics. It has never seen a literal "gravity equation" but consistently animates falling objects. Something like an implicit physics model is being learned — and that's what you want for simulation-based planning.

— Sora TR, Veo papers, simulation-as-learning camp

No — the JEPA response

Sora also routinely generates six-fingered hands, impossible gait cycles, and object disappearance. Pixel-perfect generation of surface appearance is not understanding. When the JEPA camp stress-tests these models, they fail on causal interventions the model has not seen close-enough analogues of.

— LeCun, Meta FAIR, plus some DeepMind researchers

❓ Open questions

  • Is "world model inside a diffusion transformer" a separate representation, or distributed in the weights such that we can't extract a clean one?
  • Can generative world models provide training signal for agents reliably enough to close the sim-to-real gap?
  • Do Genie-style playable worlds converge toward a general simulator, or stay locked to their training distribution?
