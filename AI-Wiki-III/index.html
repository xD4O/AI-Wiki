Volume III is a six-part tour of the research frontier as of April 2026.

The Curriculum

Twenty-one chapters in six parts. Each chapter ships with a polished SVG diagram set, a collapsible ELI5 summary, a collapsible "Further Learning" list (videos · papers · blogs · talks · code · docs), debate callouts where there's live disagreement, and a per-chapter resource list.

Part I · Architecture — Beyond the Transformer

Chapter 01

Why We're Looking Past Attention

The transformer's concrete weaknesses at 2026 scale — and the taxonomy of successors.
Chapter 02

State Space Models & Mamba

Selective SSMs, SSD, hybrids (Jamba / Samba / Griffin). When SSMs win and when they don't.
Chapter 03

Byte Latent Transformer

Meta's attack on tokenization — dynamic patches, byte-level I/O, BPE's possible twilight.
Chapter 04

The Sparse Revolution

Fine-grained MoE, BitNet b1.58, matmul-free LMs, discrete diffusion for text.

Part II · World Models & Embodied Intelligence

Chapter 05

JEPA — LeCun's Bet

I-JEPA, V-JEPA 2, energy-based models. Prediction in representation space, not pixels.
Chapter 06

Generative World Models

Sora · Veo · Genie 2/3. Does pixel-perfect generation imply understanding?
Chapter 07

Embodied Foundation Models

RT-2, π0 / π2, NVIDIA GR00T — robotics' GPT moment.
Chapter 08

Model-Based RL & Planning

Dreamer, MuZero, LLM-agent tree search as descendants.

Part III · Reasoning & Training

Chapter 09

RL on Verifiable Rewards

The R1 / o-series recipe. PRMs vs. ORMs. GRPO demystified.
Chapter 10

Inference-Time Compute

Is "think longer" a new scaling law? Best-of-N, tree search, PRMs as guides.
Chapter 11

Memory & Titans

Test-time training. Short + long + persistent memory. Continual learning.
Chapter 12

The Faithfulness Crisis

GSM-Symbolic, Anthropic's CoT studies. Is the reasoning real?

Part IV · Systems & Control new

Chapter 13

Meta-Prompting & DSPy

Prompts as source code, compiled. DSPy, APE, OPRO, TextGrad.
Chapter 14

Agentic Orchestration

LangGraph state machines, Temporal durable execution, Compound AI Systems.
Chapter 15

Deterministic Gates

Constrained decoding, JSON/grammar masking, contracts, formal gates on LLMs.

Part V · Open Problems

Chapter 16

Interpretability at the Frontier

Crosscoders, circuits in 70B+, Sleeper Agents, Alignment Faking.
Chapter 17

Alignment Beyond RLHF

Weak-to-strong, debate, RSP / FSF / Preparedness — what each commits to.
Chapter 18

The Benchmark Crisis

ARC-AGI-2, FrontierMath, METR task-horizon. Everything's saturated.

Part VI · Big Picture

Chapter 19

AI for Science

AlphaFold descendants, AI-for-math, weather, materials.
Chapter 20

Scaling Debates

Is scaling dead? The data wall, synthetic data, new scaling axes.
Chapter 21

Compute · Geopolitics · AGI

Chip stacks, energy, regulation, and the actual disagreements about timelines.

What's new in Volume III

🧒 Collapsible ELI5

Every chapter ships with a click-to-expand plain-English explanation. Read the deep dive, expand the ELI5 only when you need grounding.

📚 Curated "Further learning"

Videos, papers, blog posts, conference talks, reference code — collapsible per chapter, tagged by kind.

⚖️ Live-debate callouts

Where the field disagrees, both sides appear side-by-side with named proponents and their strongest argument.

🧪 Reference implementations

~150-line PyTorch sketches for Mamba, BLT, JEPA, Titans, GRPO, DSPy, LangGraph, constrained decoding.

📅 Timeline ribbons

A dated mini-timeline atop each chapter — when was this paper released, what followed.

❓ Open-questions box

Every chapter ends with what it couldn't settle — the reader's map of unknowns.
