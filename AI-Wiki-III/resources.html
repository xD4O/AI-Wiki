State of the research, April 2026
  • Architecture. Mamba-2 + hybrids in production. BLT (Meta, Dec 2024) is the credible "kill BPE" candidate. BitNet b1.58 is the credible 1-bit story.
  • World models. V-JEPA 2 vs. Sora/Veo/Genie 3. π2 / GR00T N2 in robotics.
  • Training. RLVR + GRPO is the recipe (DeepSeek-R1 in Nature). Inference compute is a first-class scaling axis.
  • Systems. LangGraph + Temporal-style durable execution + DSPy meta-prompting + constrained decoding are the production-default stack.
  • Safety. SAE circuits in 70B+. Sleeper Agents / Alignment Faking real. RSPs at three frontier labs. UK + US AISI as evaluators of record.
  • Benchmarks. ARC-AGI-2, FrontierMath, HLE, METR task-horizon replace MMLU / HumanEval / GSM8K.
