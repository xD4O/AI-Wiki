APPENDIX

Resources & Links

Canonical references for the foundations covered in Volume I — books, courses, papers, and implementations. Classic and evergreen first; current second.

Books

  • Deep Learning — Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville (2016). deeplearningbook.org
  • Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning — Christopher Bishop (2006). The classical ML reference.
  • Dive into Deep Learning — Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola. d2l.ai — free, interactive, PyTorch + MXNet.
  • Mathematics for Machine Learning — Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong. mml-book.github.io
  • The Elements of Statistical Learning — Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman. Stanford

Courses

  • Andrej Karpathy — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero. karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero — the best intro for modern practitioners.
  • DeepLearning.AI — Andrew Ng's platform. deeplearning.ai. Anchor specialisations on Coursera: Machine Learning Specialization, Deep Learning Specialization, NLP Specialization. Plus free short courses on prompt engineering, RAG, LangChain, LLM agents, and the weekly The Batch newsletter.
  • fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning. course.fast.ai
  • Stanford CS229 — Machine Learning. Andrew Ng. cs229.stanford.edu
  • Stanford CS231n — CNNs for Visual Recognition. cs231n.stanford.edu
  • Stanford CS224n — NLP with Deep Learning. cs224n
  • MIT 6.S191 — Intro to Deep Learning. introtodeeplearning.com

Foundational papers

  • Attention Is All You Need — Vaswani et al. (2017). arXiv:1706.03762
  • Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3) — Brown et al. (2020). arXiv:2005.14165
  • Training language models to follow instructions (InstructGPT) — Ouyang et al. (2022). arXiv:2203.02155
  • Chinchilla scaling laws — Hoffmann et al. (2022). arXiv:2203.15556
  • Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) — Rafailov et al. (2023). arXiv:2305.18290
  • Constitutional AI — Bai et al. (Anthropic 2022). arXiv:2212.08073
  • LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation — Hu et al. (2021). arXiv:2106.09685
  • BERT — Devlin et al. (2018). arXiv:1810.04805

Reference implementations

  • nanoGPT — Karpathy. github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT
  • minGPT — Karpathy. minGPT
  • llm.c — Karpathy. LLM training in raw C/CUDA. llm.c
  • Hugging Face Transformers. transformers
  • PyTorch. pytorch.org
  • JAX / Flax. jax

Video lectures

  • 3Blue1Brown — Neural Networks series. YouTube playlist — the best intuition visuals.
  • Karpathy — Let's build GPT from scratch. YouTube
  • Yannic Kilcher — ML paper walkthroughs. YouTube
  • Machine Learning Street Talk. YouTube
